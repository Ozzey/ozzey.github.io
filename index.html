I am a robotics and reinforcement learning researcher working on manipulation problems, in particular making the control smarter, safer and more precise.

I started at MBZUAI as a Research Engineer and am now a master's student in the Robotics Department and AIRoCS Lab. Previously, I worked with the Center for Cognitive Modeling at MIPT, where I completed my BSc in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science.
